# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, The species of an act is taken from its object, as stated above (I-II:18:2). Now the object or matter of liberality is money and whatever has a money value, as stated in the foregoing Article (Reply to Objection 2). And since every virtue is consistent with its object, it follows that, since liberality is a virtue, its act is consistent with money. Now money comes under the head of useful goods, since all external goods are directed to man's use. Hence the proper act of liberality is making use of money or riches.[^newadvent]
