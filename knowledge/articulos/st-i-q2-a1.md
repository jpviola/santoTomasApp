# Summary

A proposition can be self-evident in itself but not to us. 'God exists' is self-evident in itself, because in God essence and existence are the same, but it is not self-evident to us, since we do not know God's essence; it must be demonstrated from effects better known to us. Aquinas thereby rejects the argument later called ontological.[^newadvent]
