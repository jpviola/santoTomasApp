# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Order always has reference to some principle. Wherefore since there are many kinds of principle—namely, according to site, as a point; according to intellect, as the principle of demonstration; and according to each individual cause —so are there many kinds of order. Now principle, according to origin, without priority, exists in God as we have stated (I:33:1: so there must likewise be order according to origin, without priority; and this is called 'the order of nature ': in the words of Augustine (Contra Maxim. iv): "Not whereby one is prior to another, but whereby one is from another."[^newadvent]
