# Summary

There are four cardinal virtues: prudence perfects reason itself; justice rectifies operations toward others; temperance moderates the passions that draw us away from reason; fortitude strengthens us against passions that hold us back from following reason.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
