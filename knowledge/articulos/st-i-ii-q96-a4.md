# Summary

Just laws bind in conscience. Laws are unjust when contrary to the human good (by their end, author or form) or contrary to the divine good. Laws of the first kind are acts of violence rather than laws and do not bind in conscience, except perhaps to avoid scandal or disturbance; laws of the second kind must never be obeyed.[^newadvent]
