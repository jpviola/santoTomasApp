# Summary

It is lawful and necessary for human life that man possess property as his own, as regards the power to procure and dispense things, because people care more for what is theirs, affairs are better ordered, and peace is better kept. But as regards use, man ought to possess external things as common, readily sharing them with others in need.[^newadvent]
