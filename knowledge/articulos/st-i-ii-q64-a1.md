# Summary

Moral virtue consists in a mean between excess and defect, measured by right reason. The mean is not mediocrity but conformity with the rule of reason; with respect to its conformity to reason, virtue is an extreme.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
