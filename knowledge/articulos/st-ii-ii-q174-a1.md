# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, The species of moral habits and acts are distinguished according to their objects. Now the object of prophecy is something known by God and surpassing the faculty of man. Wherefore, according to the difference of such things, prophecy is divided into various species, as assigned above. Now it has been stated above (II-II:71:6 ad 2) that the future is contained in the Divine knowledge in two ways. First, as in its cause: and thus we have the prophecy of " denunciation," which is not always fulfilled. but it foretells the relation of cause to effect, which is sometimes hindered by some other occurrence supervening.
