# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, It is impossible for God to be seen by the sense of sight, or by any other sense, or faculty of the sensitive power. For every such kind of power is the act of a corporeal organ, as will be shown later (I:78. Now act is proportional to the nature which possesses it. Hence no power of that kind can go beyond corporeal things. For God is incorporeal, as was shown above (I:3:1). Hence He cannot be seen by the sense or the imagination, but only by the intellect.[^newadvent]
