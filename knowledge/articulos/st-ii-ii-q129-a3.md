# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, The essence of human virtue consists in safeguarding the good of reason in human affairs, for this is man's proper good. Now among external human things honors take precedence of all others, as stated above (Article 1; I-II:11:2, Objection 3). Therefore magnanimity, which observes the mode of reason in great honors, is a virtue.[^newadvent]
