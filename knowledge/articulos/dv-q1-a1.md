# Summary

Aquinas derives the transcendentals (thing, one, something, good, true) from being. The true adds to being a relation of conformity to the intellect; hence truth is defined as the adequation of thing and intellect (adaequatio rei et intellectus), and knowledge is an effect of truth.[^corpus-thomisticum]
