# Summary

The intellect is a power of the soul and not the very essence of the soul. Only in God is understanding identical with his being; in creatures the power of understanding is distinct from their essence.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
