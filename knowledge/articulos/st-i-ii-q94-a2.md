# Summary

The first principle of practical reason is that good is to be done and pursued and evil avoided (bonum est faciendum et prosequendum, et malum vitandum). The precepts of natural law follow the order of natural inclinations: to preserve one's being, to the union of male and female and the education of offspring, and, proper to reason, to know the truth about God and to live in society.[^newadvent]
