# Summary

Charity is the form of all the virtues because it directs the acts of all other virtues to the last end, God. It is not their exemplar or essential form but their efficient and final principle.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
