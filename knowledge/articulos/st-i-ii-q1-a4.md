# Summary

There cannot be an infinite series of ends, since then nothing would be desired and no action would begin or terminate. There is therefore an ultimate end of human life, for the sake of which all else is desired.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
