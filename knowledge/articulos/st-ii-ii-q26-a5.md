# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Out of charity we ought to love more that which has more fully the reason for being loved out of charity, as stated above (Article 2; II-II:25:12). Now fellowship in the full participation of happiness which is the reason for loving one's neighbor, is a greater reason for loving, than the participation of happiness by way of overflow, which is the reason for loving one's own body. Therefore, as regards the welfare of the soul we ought to love our neighbor more than our own body.[^newadvent]
