# Summary

Art is right reason about things to be made (recta ratio factibilium); prudence is right reason about things to be done (recta ratio agibilium). Prudence requires a right appetite toward ends, so it is a moral as well as an intellectual virtue, whereas art does not require a good will.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
