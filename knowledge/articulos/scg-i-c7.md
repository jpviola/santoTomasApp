# Summary

Aquinas holds that what reason naturally knows cannot be contrary to the truth of faith, since both come from God, the author of our nature and of revelation. Arguments brought against the faith therefore do not proceed from first principles known by nature and can be shown to be either false or not necessary.[^corpus-thomisticum]
