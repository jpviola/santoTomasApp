# Summary

Man is by nature a social and political animal, living in a multitude, because he cannot provide sufficiently for his life alone and has language to communicate with others. A multitude needs a governing principle directed to the common good; a government is just when it seeks the common good and unjust when it seeks the private good of the ruler.[^corpus-thomisticum]
