# Summary

The object of justice is the right (ius), that is, the just thing itself: a work adjusted to another according to a certain equality. Law is not right itself but a certain rule or expression of right.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
