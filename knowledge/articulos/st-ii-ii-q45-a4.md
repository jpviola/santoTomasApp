# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, The wisdom which is a gift of the Holy Ghost, as stated above (Article 1), enables us to judge aright of Divine things, or of other things according to Divine rules, by reason of a certain connaturalness or union with Divine things, which is the effect of charity, as stated above (Article 2; II-II:23:5). Hence the wisdom of which we are speaking presupposes charity. Now charity is incompatible with mortal sin, as shown above (II-II:24:12). Therefore it follows that the wisdom of which we are speaking cannot be together with mortal sin.[^newadvent]
