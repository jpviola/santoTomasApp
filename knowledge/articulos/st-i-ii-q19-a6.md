# Summary

Following an erring conscience excuses from sin only if the error is involuntary and not culpable. If the ignorance is voluntary, directly or through negligence, the act done in accordance with it is not excused.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
