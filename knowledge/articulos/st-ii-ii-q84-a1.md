# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Adoration is directed to the reverence of the person adored. Now it is evident from what we have said (II-II:81:2,4) that it is proper to religion to show reverence to God. Hence the adoration whereby we adore God is an act of religion.[^newadvent]
