# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Just as "guile" consists in the execution of craftiness, so also does " fraud." But they seem to differ in the fact that "guile" belongs in general to the execution of craftiness, whether this be effected by words, or by deeds, whereas " fraud " belongs more properly to the execution of craftiness by deeds.[^newadvent]
