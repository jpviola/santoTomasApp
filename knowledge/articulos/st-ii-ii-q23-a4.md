# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Acts and habits are specified by their objects, as shown above (I-II:18:2; I-II:54:2). Now the proper object of love is the good, as stated above (I-II:27:1), so that wherever there is a special aspect of good, there is a special kind of love. But the Divine good, inasmuch as it is the object of happiness, has a special aspect of good, wherefore the love of charity, which is the love of that good, is a special kind of love. Therefore charity is a special virtue.[^newadvent]
