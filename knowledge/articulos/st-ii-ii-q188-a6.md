# Summary

The contemplative life is simply better than the active life, but the work of teaching and preaching that flows from the fullness of contemplation is better than contemplation alone: as it is better to illuminate than merely to shine, it is better to contemplate and give to others the fruits of contemplation (contemplari et contemplata aliis tradere).[^newadvent]
