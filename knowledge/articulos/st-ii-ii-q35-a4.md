# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, As stated above (I-II:84:3; I-II:84:4), a capital vice is one which easily gives rise to others as being their final cause. Now just as we do many things on account of pleasure, both in order to obtain it, and through being moved to do something under the impulse of pleasure, so again we do many things on account of sorrow, either that we may avoid it, or through being exasperated into doing something under pressure thereof. Wherefore, since sloth is a kind of sorrow, as stated above (Article 2; I-II:85:8), it is fittingly reckoned a capital sin.[^newadvent]
