# Summary

After showing that happiness consists neither in wealth, honor, fame, power, bodily goods nor pleasure, Aquinas concludes that no created good can constitute happiness. Happiness is the perfect good that fully satisfies desire, and the object of the will is universal good, found only in God.[^newadvent]
