# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, As is plain from what has been said in I-II:35:7, for true bodily pain are required bodily hurt and the sense of hurt. Now Christ's body was able to be hurt, since it was passible and mortal, as above stated (III:14:1 and III:14:2); neither was the sense of hurt wanting to it, since Christ's soul possessed perfectly all natural powers. Therefore no one should doubt but that in Christ there was true pain.[^newadvent]
