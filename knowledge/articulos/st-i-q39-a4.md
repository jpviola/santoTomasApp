# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Some have said that this name "God" and the like, properly according to their nature, stand for the essence, but by reason of some notional adjunct are made to stand for the Person. This opinion apparently arose from considering the divine simplicity, which requires that in God, He "who possesses" and "what is possessed" be the same. So He who possesses Godhead, which is signified by the name God, is the same as Godhead. But when we consider the proper way of expressing ourselves, the mode of signification must be considered no less than the thing signified. Hence as this word " God " signifies the divine essence as in Him Who possesses it, just as the name " man " signifies humanity in a subject, others more truly have said that this word " God," from its mode of signification, can, in its proper sense, stand for person, as does the word " man."
