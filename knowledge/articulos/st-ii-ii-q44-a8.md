# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, As stated above (Article 4, Reply to Objection 1), the mode which is essential to an act of virtue comes under the precept which prescribes that virtuous act. Now the order of charity is essential to the virtue, since it is based on the proportion of love to the thing beloved, as shown above (II-II:25:12; II-II:26:1 and II-II:26:2). It is therefore evident that the order of charity must come under the precept.[^newadvent]
