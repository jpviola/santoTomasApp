# Summary

Every human law has the nature of law insofar as it is derived from the natural law, either as a conclusion from premises (such as 'do not kill') or as a determination of something general (such as the particular penalty for a crime). A law that deviates from natural law is no longer a law but a perversion of law.[^newadvent]
