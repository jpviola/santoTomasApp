# Summary

Man has free will because he acts from judgment that is not natural instinct but a comparison made by reason. Since reason can consider contingent particulars in different ways, it is not determined to one course, and man is free. Otherwise counsels, exhortations, commands, rewards and punishments would be in vain.[^newadvent]
