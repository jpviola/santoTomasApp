# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Chastity takes its name from the fact that reason "chastises" concupiscence, which, like a child, needs curbing, as the Philosopher states (Ethic. iii, 12). Now the essence of human virtue consists in being something moderated by reason, as shown above (I-II:64:1). Therefore it is evident that chastity is a virtue.[^newadvent]
