# Summary

Sacred doctrine argues from the articles of faith as its principles and can answer objections against them. Because grace does not destroy nature but perfects it (gratia non tollit naturam, sed perficit), natural reason is placed at the service of faith, and the philosophers may be cited where they reached the truth by reason.[^newadvent]
