# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, As stated above (II-II:123:1 and II-II:123:3), it belongs to virtue to safeguard man in the good of reason. Now the good of reason consists in the truth as its proper object, and in justice as its proper effect, as shown above (II-II:109:1 and II-II:109:2; 123, 12). And martyrdom consists essentially in standing firmly to truth and justice against the assaults of persecution. Hence it is evident that martyrdom is an act of virtue.[^newadvent]
