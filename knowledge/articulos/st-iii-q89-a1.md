# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Sins are pardoned through Penance, as stated above (III:86:1. But there can be no remission of sins except through the infusion of grace. Wherefore it follows that grace is infused into man through Penance. Now all the gratuitous virtues flow from grace, even as all the powers result from the essence of the soul; as stated in I-II:110:4 ad 1. Therefore all the virtues are restored through Penance.[^newadvent]
