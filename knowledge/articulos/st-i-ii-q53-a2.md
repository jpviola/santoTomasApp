# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Habits diminish, just as they increase, in two ways, as we have already explained (I-II:52:1. And since they increase through the same cause as that which engenders them, so too they diminish by the same cause as that which corrupts them: since the diminishing of a habit is the road which leads to its corruption, even as, on the other hand, the engendering of a habit is a foundation of its increase.[^newadvent]
