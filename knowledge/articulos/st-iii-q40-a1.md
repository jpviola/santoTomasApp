# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, Christ's manner of life had to be in keeping with the end of His Incarnation, by reason of which He came into the world. Now He came into the world, first, that He might publish the truth. thus He says Himself (John 18:37): "For this was I born, and for this came I into the world, that I should give testimony to the truth." Hence it was fitting not that He should hide Himself by leading a solitary life, but that He should appear openly and preach in public. Wherefore (Luke 4:42-43) He says to those who wished to stay Him: "To other cities also I must preach the kingdom of God: for therefore am I sent." Secondly, He came in order to free men from sin; according to 1 Timothy 1:15: " Christ Jesus came into this world to save sinners."
