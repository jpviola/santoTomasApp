# Respondeo (excerpt)

I answer that, A sacrifice properly so called is something done for that honor which is properly due to God, in order to appease Him: and hence it is that Augustine says (De Civ. Dei x): "A true sacrifice is every good work done in order that we may cling to God in holy fellowship, yet referred to that consummation of happiness wherein we can be truly blessed." But, as is added in the same place, " Christ offered Himself up for us in the Passion ": and this voluntary enduring of the Passion was most acceptable to God, as coming from charity.
