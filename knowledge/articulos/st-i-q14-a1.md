# Summary

Knowing beings differ from non-knowing beings in that they can possess the form of another thing as well as their own. Immateriality is thus the reason why something is cognitive, and the degree of knowledge follows the degree of immateriality; God, supremely immaterial, is supremely knowing.[^newadvent]
