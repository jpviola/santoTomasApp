# Summary

The five ways proceed from motion, efficient causality, contingency, gradation of perfection, and the governance of things. Each argues that an essentially ordered series cannot proceed to infinity and must depend on a first: an unmoved mover, a first efficient cause, a necessary being, a maximum in being and goodness, and an intelligence directing natural things to their end. To the objection from evil he replies, with Augustine, that God would not permit evil unless he could bring good out of it.[^newadvent]
