# Summary

Truth is found primarily in the intellect and secondarily in things, insofar as they are related to an intellect. Truth is the conformity (adaequatio) of intellect and thing; natural things are true in relation to the divine intellect that measures them.[^newadvent]

[^newadvent]: Summa Theologiae, trad. inglesa de los dominicos (1920)
