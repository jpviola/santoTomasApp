# Summary

Aquinas accepts the jurists' definition of justice as the perpetual and constant will to render to each his right (perpetua et constans voluntas ius suum unicuique tribuendi), and restates it: justice is a habit whereby a man renders to each his due by a constant and perpetual will.[^newadvent]
