# Summary

Unbelievers who have never received the faith, such as pagans and Jews, must in no way be compelled to believe, because belief is an act of the will. Aquinas nevertheless holds, in line with his time, that heretics and apostates who once accepted the faith may be compelled to fulfill what they promised.[^newadvent]
